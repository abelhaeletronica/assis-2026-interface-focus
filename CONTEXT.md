## Article
**Title:** Stigmergy and Perishable Traces: Clay as a Material Operator 
for Rethinking Collective Coordination

**Journal:** Interface Focus — Royal Society Publishing  
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20277885

## Core Argument
Stigmergy should not be understood merely as an efficient coordination 
mechanism based on environmental marks, but as a material process whose 
dynamics depend on the precariousness, deterioration, and continuous 
reactivation of traces. Impermanence is not a defect — it is the 
operative condition of stigmergic systems. Raw clay makes this visible.

## Five Conceptual Displacements
1. From information to materiality
2. From stability to maintenance
3. From efficiency to friction
4. From optimisation to precarious temporalities
5. From permanent marker to perishable trace
